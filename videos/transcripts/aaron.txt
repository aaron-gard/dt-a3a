How might we build a structured AI training program that equips business users with confidence in their critical thinking and technical ability so that human AI co-creativity becomes a genuine individual capability. Firstly, it must provide a structured training program so that every staff member completes a defined AI training program.

So that co-creativity becomes an individual skill applied at all client work.
